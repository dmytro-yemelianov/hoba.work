# Rev. Proc. 2025-28 (IRS)

IRS guidance on the 2025 restoration: foreign research or experimental expenditures keep the 15-year amortisation period; balances capitalised under the 2022–2024 rules may be recovered in full in the first tax year beginning after 31 December 2024 or ratably over two years; and a taxpayer meeting the section 448(c) gross-receipts test — $31,000,000 average annual gross receipts over the three prior tax years for a year beginning in 2025 — may elect the new treatment retroactively to tax years beginning after 31 December 2021, by 6 July 2026.
