# Promissory Estoppel and Pre-Employment Good Faith (Restatement of Employment Law § 2.02)

Section 2.02 of the Restatement of Employment Law (American Law Institute, 2015) and prevailing common-law promissory estoppel doctrines establish actionable employer liability when an employer makes representations of an existing, funded position to induce a prospective employee to incur detrimental reliance (e.g. resigning from prior employment, incurring moving costs) while knowing the underlying headcount authorization or budgetary certification had not been secured or had lapsed.
