# Layoffs and discharges, information sector (BLS JOLTS)

Official separations in the US information sector were 451,000 in 2022, 399,000 in 2023, 395,000 in 2024 and 447,000 in 2025 — roughly flat across the period, and without the year-to-year collapse reported by tracked-layoff counts over the same span.
