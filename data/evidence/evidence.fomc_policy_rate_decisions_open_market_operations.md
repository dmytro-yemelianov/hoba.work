# FOMC policy rate decisions (Open Market Operations)

The FOMC raised the federal funds target range to 5.25–5.50% effective 27 July 2023, the peak of the tightening cycle, and held it there until the 25 basis point reduction of 18 September 2024 — roughly fourteen months.
