# 26 U.S.C. §174A — domestic research expenditures

Section 70302(a) of Public Law 119-21, enacted 4 July 2025, added section 174A, which allows a deduction for domestic research or experimental expenditures paid or incurred during the taxable year notwithstanding section 263, with an election to capitalise and amortise them over not less than 60 months instead. Effective for amounts paid or incurred in tax years beginning after 31 December 2024.
