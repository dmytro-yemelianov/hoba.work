# Salary Transparency: Growth Slows, but Momentum Continues (Indeed Hiring Lab)

Tracks the share of US job postings on Indeed that disclose pay: 57.8% in September 2024, up from 52.2% a year earlier, with wide variation by occupation. Roughly four in ten postings still omit compensation — the structural precondition for compensation-band discrepancies surfacing only at late stages.
