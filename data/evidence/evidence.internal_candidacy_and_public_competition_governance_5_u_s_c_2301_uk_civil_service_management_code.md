# Internal Candidacy and Public Competition Governance (5 U.S.C. § 2301 & UK Civil Service Management Code)

5 U.S.C. § 2301 (Merit system principles) and the UK Civil Service Management Code (Section 1.1 on fair and open competition) require transparent declaration of whether positions are open exclusively to internal promotion slates or contested in open public competition. Open competitions running parallel to pre-identified incumbent candidacies must state evaluation rules explicitly to prevent deceptive solicitation of external applicant labor.
