# Applications per opening (Greenhouse hiring benchmarks)

Applications received per open role across the reporting platform's customer base rose from 116 in 2022 to 244 in 2025, an increase of 111%. The stated dataset covers over 6,000 companies and over 640 million applications, so it measures one platform's customers rather than the labour market.
