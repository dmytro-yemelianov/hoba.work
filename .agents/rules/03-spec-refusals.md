# Spec Refusals & Modelling Disciplines (SPEC-MODEL §8)

1. **Shapes first, amounts only with evidence**:
   - The shape of a funding chain, workflow, or mechanism is always expressible.
   - Exact numbers, fee percentages, or rate cards must carry an explicit evidence citation (`evidence_ids: ["EVD-xxx"]`) or remain absent/zero.

2. **No probabilistic forecasting**:
   - The model expresses what happened or what was permitted to occur; it never computes probabilities of a hire.

3. **Knowability, never knowledge**:
   - The model bounds what a party *could* know from their lossy projection. It never asserts what a party actually *knew* or *believed*.

4. **No motive attribution**:
   - Conditions carry owners, determinacy, and parameters; they never carry subjective intent, blame, or hidden motives.
