# Compensation Double Bind

Candidate is required to state salary expectation first; quoting high triggers immediate rejection, while quoting low triggers leveling downranking.

### Trigger Rule
Asymmetric salary inquiry where employer refuses band disclosure prior to candidate submitting binding number.

### What this Establishes
- Information asymmetry is utilized as an uncalibrated gatekeeper before technical merit is evaluated.

### What this Does NOT Establish
- Does not mean employer has unlimited budget or malice.
