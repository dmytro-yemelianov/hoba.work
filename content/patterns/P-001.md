# Seniority Double Bind

Candidate is simultaneously rejected as overqualified for junior/mid roles and underqualified (or insufficiently specialized) for staff/leadership roles.

### Trigger Rule
Candidate observes rejection for overqualification at level N and skill-depth shortfall at level N+1 within the same market cycle.

### What this Establishes
- Candidate falls into an evaluation boundary gap where compensation/experience expectations mismatch standard job tiers.

### What this Does NOT Establish
- Does not establish that the candidate lacks market value.
- Does not establish malice or coordinated collusion between employers.
