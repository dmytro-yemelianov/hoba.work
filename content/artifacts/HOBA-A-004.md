# Materially similar role reposted shortly after rejection

Candidate observes the same or near-identical job listing reposted or refreshed on job boards within 1–8 weeks of rejection.

### Diagnostic Non-Inferences
- Does not establish that the role was a "fake" or "ghost" posting.
- Does not establish that the prior interview feedback was fraudulent.
