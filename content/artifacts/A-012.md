# Unsolicited recruiter outreach followed by ghosting

Recruiter opens direct outreach expressing strong interest, then stops replying once the candidate answers with availability.

### Diagnostic Non-Inferences
- Does not prove the profile was rejected; often it is what an automated batch sourcing campaign looks like.
