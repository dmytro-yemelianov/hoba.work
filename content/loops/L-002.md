# Take-Home Opportunity-Cost Saturation Loop

Candidates spend heavy hours on take-home assignments with no bounded scope, which leaves less time for applications, while reviewers work through the queue under evaluation fatigue.

### Cycle Dynamics
This causal loop reinforces mechanisms across iterations:
- `mech.interview_resource_scheduling_saturation` amplifies `mech.take_home_evaluation_fatigue_asymmetry`
- `mech.take_home_evaluation_fatigue_asymmetry` amplifies `mech.interview_resource_scheduling_saturation`
