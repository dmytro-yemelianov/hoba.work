# Inflated-Requirements Search Saturation Loop

Teams write inflated requirements, filtering out strong generalists, prolonging open search, causing recruiter fatigue and mid-process role redefinitions.

- `M-024` amplifies `M-008`
- `M-008` amplifies `M-013`
