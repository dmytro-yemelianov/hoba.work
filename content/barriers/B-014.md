# Outbound Sourcing & Talent Pool Contact

Recruiter-initiated outreach to passive candidates and talent-pool building, independent of any inbound application.

### Pass Condition
The candidate responds to outreach and is attached to an active, authorised requisition rather than a speculative pool.
