# Upfront Compensation Band Disclosure

Publish verified salary and equity bands directly in job descriptions or disclose them prior to scheduling technical interview rounds.

### Expected Effects
- M-004 has nothing to operate on: the band is stated before the candidate is asked for a number
- B-009 becomes arithmetic rather than a negotiation about whether a band exists

### Measurements
- `late_stage_compensation_drop_rate`
- `screen_to_offer_band_agreement_rate`
