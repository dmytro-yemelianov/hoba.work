# Interview Seats Booked With Prep Time and a Second Name on the Rota

Book every interview seat as preparation plus session, take those hours off the sprint board, and give each round a second name on a standing rota so an unavailable interviewer passes the seat rather than moves the date.

### Expected Effects
- mech.interview_resource_scheduling_saturation loses one of its two outputs: a seat that cannot be filled moves the seat, not the date
- A round at bar.technical_screen_live_assessment is booked with reading time attached to it, so the material is opened before the session or the seat passes to the second name
- Interviewer hours sit on the sprint board as committed work rather than as an overrun absorbed by whoever had the slot

### Measurements
- `interview_reschedule_rate`
- `panel_seat_backfill_rate`
- `rounds_per_interviewer_month`
