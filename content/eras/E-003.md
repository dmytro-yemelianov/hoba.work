# Rates up, payroll repriced

Four things moved at once and in the same direction: the cost of money, the
value of the equity, the availability of new funding, and the tax treatment of
the payroll itself. None of them was a judgement about anybody's work.

The change worth carrying forward is not the size of the reduction. It is what
happened to what a job posting *is*. When hiring narrows to replacing specific
departures, a posting stops describing a plan and starts describing a person who
recently left — and the evidence that satisfies it narrows to the same title, at
similar scale, in the same stack. That is a different document to apply to, and
most of the atlas is about what applying to it does.
