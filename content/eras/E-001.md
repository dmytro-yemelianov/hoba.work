# Zero rates and a same-year deduction

Two policy facts, held for over a decade, set the price of a hire. Money cost
almost nothing to borrow, and a developer's salary reduced taxable income in the
year it was paid. Neither was about hiring, and both decided how hiring worked.

The consequence for a candidate was specific: an empty seat cost more than a
wrong hire, so employers absorbed the risk of training. Nothing in this era
required an employer to be generous for that to be true.
